[0:22:14] Noah Labhart: Let's flip the script a little bit. Tell me about a mistake you made and how you and your team responded to it.

[0:22:20] Mrinal Wadhwa: The world around agents is evolving rapidly and understanding of what is the right way to build these types of applications is also evolving very rapidly. We made several kind of architectural choices that had to be corrected. For example, initially we thought that we will have long-term memory and we will keep the entire history of the conversation an agent is having with its LLM in a loop. And we keep feeding that history back to the LLM. And it seemed to work initially. Like in our initial prototypes, when you have agents that only need to do, only need to autonomously take five or 10 independent steps, that approach works really well. But if an agent needs to take 500 independent steps, if you keep all the history of the conversation it's been having with its LLM and with its tools, and you feed it back in, 10, 20, 30 iterations in, it's too much information to feed to an LLM. It's too noisy. And there's a term now in the industry around it, like it creates context rot. So what ends up happening is that the LLM gets confused and makes a wrong turn. And it makes a wrong turn and then makes another wrong turn. The problem continues to compound and the agent is never able to achieve its goal. So the learning from that was actually what we need to do is prune and trim very carefully what we send to the LLM in each iteration of the agent's loop. And if we do that well, we can have agents that are able to coherently work towards their goal over many hours. So I earlier gave that example of that interview agent that conducts first-round voice interviews. What we saw was like, you know, it'll ask two good questions or three good questions But at the fourth or fifth question, it would start to steer the interview in some completely incorrect direction. And the reason was it had too much noise in its context. And so once we had this realization that we need to be careful about what we put in, we were able to run hour-long interviews. So this is just one example of the type of mistake we made along the way, but there were several others.
