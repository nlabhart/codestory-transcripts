And then we started deploying a lot of algorithms to adjust that and to make it better, to clusterize those signals, to find different locations that look very similar because of the similarity of the Wi-Fi signals, etc. etc. And we ended up building a solution that in 2014, for example, we presented it in Berlin in a conference that was sponsored by ACM and also Microsoft Research. And we tested it against many other indoor location technologies. And we ended up with a pretty good result, reaching 2.8 meter accuracy, which is about 30 times more accurate than the GPS.
